**The advertised deal is $1 per month for three months, not $1 total.** On October 1, 2026, [Shopify's U.S. pricing page](https://www.shopify.com/pricing) advertised three days free followed by three months at $1/month. Your actual offer depends on eligibility and location; confirm the currency, plan, and dates shown during signup.

The useful question is what your store will cost when the discount ends. This guide focuses on that billing decision. For the setup sequence, use our [complete free-trial guide](/blog/shopify-free-trial-2026-complete-guide).

## What is the Shopify $1/month promotion?

The free trial and the paid promotional period are separate stages. Shopify's [trial documentation](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/intro-to-shopify/pricing-plans/free-trial) says trial length and promotional pricing can vary, and promotions apply to eligible merchants and plans. Do not assume an old screenshot or a partner link guarantees your offer.

This is a Shopify promotion, not an exclusive AdsX discount. [Check the current signup offer](https://www.shopify.com/free-trial), then record the details in the confirmation flow. If the expected price is absent, resolve the discrepancy before selecting a paid plan.

## How to check the offer before confirming

Create a small billing record you can use again at renewal:

| Item to capture | What to write down | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Currency and promotional price | The actual amount shown | A dollar example is not a worldwide price promise |
| Selected plan | The plan's exact name | Compare the package you will actually use |
| Promotion dates | First charge and discount end | Set a review reminder before renewal |
| Normal renewal | Amount and billing frequency | Compare monthly cash needs with an annual commitment |
| Extras | App subscriptions, usage, domain, theme | Keep the plan discount separate from other purchases |

Keep the confirmation alongside a dated screenshot. That gives you a concrete reference if your expectations and the first invoice differ. Shopify's [billing charge overview](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/your-account/manage-billing/billing-charges) separates recurring, usage-based, and one-time charges.

## A worked budget: subscription price versus store cost

Here is an illustrative budget, **not a Shopify price quote**. Assume a merchant receives three $1 promotional months, then budgets a hypothetical $45 monthly plan price for nine more months. Add an optional $12/month app and an $18 domain purchase:

| Illustrative expense | Calculation | Twelve paid months |
|---|---|---|
| Promotional subscription | 3 × $1 | $3 |
| Remaining subscription | 9 × $45 | $405 |
| Optional app | 12 × $12 | $144 |
| Domain | One $18 purchase | $18 |
| **Subtotal** | $3 + $405 + $144 + $18 | **$570** |

That subtotal excludes taxes, payment processing, inventory, fulfillment, returns, and advertising. Replace every assumption with your own quote in the [startup cost calculator](/tools/shopify-startup-cost-calculator). Then use the [10, 50, and 100 order budget](/blog/shopify-store-budget-10-50-100-orders) to see how operating expenses change with sales.

The discount is useful only within a budget you can sustain. Saving on the subscription does not establish product demand or make an optional app necessary.

## What happens if you switch plans or billing cycles?

Shopify's [plan-change billing documentation](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/your-account/manage-billing/managing-your-bills/billing-plan-changes) says an eligible tier change during a promotion keeps its original end date, with the applicable prorated promotional billing. It does not grant a fresh three-month promotion.

The same guidance says a switch from monthly to annual billing during the promotion takes effect after it ends, with the annual amount charged upfront. Check **Settings → Plan** for the scheduled change. Our [annual versus monthly cash-flow guide](/blog/shopify-annual-vs-monthly-billing-cash-flow) helps compare that payment with other launch expenses.

## What should you check before canceling?

Follow Shopify's [store cancellation instructions](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/your-account/manage-orgs-and-stores/manage-pricing-plan/deactivate-store). Outstanding charges can still be collected, and externally billed app subscriptions need separate attention. “Cancel anytime” should not be read as a promise that every bill disappears or a payment will be refunded.

Before the promotion ends, review three things: whether checkout and fulfillment work, whether customers have shown meaningful demand, and whether normal recurring costs fit your budget. The [trial evaluation scorecard](/blog/shopify-free-trial-evaluation-scorecard) gives you a practical record for that decision.
