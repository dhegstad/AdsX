There is no single longest Shopify free trial available to everyone. The standard trial, a discounted subscription, an eligibility-based program, and a development environment have different terms and purposes. Choose the route you genuinely qualify for and compare the normal cost after any offer ends.

Reviewed September 9, 2026: Shopify's public standard pages show a three-day free trial and a subsequent promotional paid period. The current 1MBB page describes a discounted six-month Basic offer for eligible merchants in the US and Canada, rather than the historical 120-day free-trial description. Verify the amount and terms in the applicable enrollment flow.

## Compare the routes honestly

| Route | What it is | What to verify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard Shopify trial | A temporary evaluation period | Current duration and start time |
| Standard promotional subscription | A discounted paid plan period | Currency, eligible plan, duration, renewal |
| 1MBB program offer | An eligibility-based program benefit | Enrollment, location, and current offer terms |
| Development environment | A workspace for supported development/testing use | Restrictions and the correct live-store transition |

Do not add free days and discounted months together and call the entire period free. A $1 subscription is inexpensive, but it is still a paid subscription.

## The standard route

Review the [current free-trial page](https://www.shopify.com/free-trial) and [trial documentation](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/intro-to-shopify/pricing-plans/free-trial). The trial starts when you sign up, so prepare a product and a short requirement list first.

Use the evaluation period to determine whether the store can handle your products, payments, delivery, and operations. If you need an app, include its pricing and setup requirements. A separate app trial can end on a different date from the store trial.

Our [complete trial guide](/blog/shopify-free-trial-2026-complete-guide) explains the stages and the additional charges that can matter.

## The current 1MBB offer

Shopify's [1MBB program page](https://www.shopify.com/1mbb) directs Black entrepreneurs to Operation HOPE and describes a discounted Basic-plan period of six months. The page limits the offer to merchants in the US and Canada and describes a US-only card-reader benefit.

Do not assume the older 120-day free offer still applies to a new enrollment. Keep the current written offer and confirm its billing amount, currency, plan, duration, and normal renewal price before starting.

Go through the official program route. A standard affiliate signup link is not a substitute for enrollment and does not establish eligibility. See the [detailed 1MBB update](/blog/shopify-120-day-free-trial-black-owned-businesses) for the questions to verify.

## Development stores are not a free retail loophole

Shopify maintains different store types for development and client work. Use the official [store-type documentation](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/stores) to understand their supported purposes and restrictions.

A development workspace should not be advertised as a permanent free live shop. If you are a merchant evaluating a real business, choose the appropriate merchant or client-transfer route and verify how it becomes a live store.

The useful question is whether the environment supports the work you are doing. Unlimited time in a restricted environment does not equal unlimited live selling at no subscription cost.

## Get more useful work from the time you have

Prepare the parts that do not require a store first: product information, a supplier sample, delivery assumptions, a list of must-have functions, and a budget. Then use Shopify to test the requirements that need the actual platform.

A simple sequence is:

1. Define the product and customer offer.
2. Build one representative product page.
3. Verify payment and delivery requirements.
4. Test the order workflow appropriately.
5. Record the normal operating cost and decide whether to proceed.

Use the [trial scorecard](/blog/shopify-free-trial-evaluation-scorecard) to keep that decision concrete. Spending an extra week choosing a font does not necessarily improve the evidence for the platform choice.

## What if the trial ends before you are ready?

Check the current account state and Shopify's documented options. Do not assume an extension is guaranteed, or that cancelling and creating another account restarts the same promotion.

If an essential requirement remains unresolved, identify it before paying for a longer period. If the platform fits but you need more build time, compare the normal subscription cost with the value of continuing the work.

A store that requires a verified integration may need a longer evaluation than a small standard catalog. That is a reason to plan the test and budget clearly, not to claim that everyone needs the longest possible promotion.

## Compare offers on the same basis

Record total subscription payments during the period, additional tools, normal renewal cost, and any eligibility restrictions. A longer discounted period can be valuable, but it should not outweigh a failed business requirement.

Use the route that fits your situation, preserve the written terms, and launch when the store passes its checks. Special offers are a budgeting input; they are not evidence that a store is ready or a product has demand.
