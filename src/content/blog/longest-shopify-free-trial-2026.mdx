The best runway for *most* people is the **3-day trial then $1/month for 3 months** (cheap, and you can sell immediately). Want unlimited free *build* time? A **Partner development store** never expires — but can't take real orders. Qualifying Black-owned businesses get a genuine **[120-day free trial](/blog/shopify-120-day-free-trial-black-owned-businesses)**. "Shopify free trial" is one of the most-searched terms in commerce, and most articles bury the lede: the *standard* trial is now just **3 days**. That's barely enough to poke around, let alone build a store. The good news is there are several legitimate ways to get far more time — they just suit different situations, and one popular option comes with a catch worth understanding before you start.

Here's every route, compared honestly.

## Every route to a longer Shopify trial (2026)

| Route | How long | Who qualifies | Can you sell? | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Standard trial** | 3 days | Anyone | Yes | A quick look before committing |
| **$1/month for 3 months** | ~3 months near-free | Anyone (after the trial) | **Yes** | Most new stores validating a real idea |
| **Partner development store** | Unlimited (no deadline) | Anyone (free Partner account) | **No** — build/test only | Builders & agencies who want to build first |
| **Affiliate/partner 44-day trial** | ~44 days | Via some affiliate/partner links | Yes | Occasional extended offers |
| **1MBB program** | **120 days** | ≥50% Black-owned, US/CA/UK | Yes | [Eligible founders](/blog/shopify-120-day-free-trial-black-owned-businesses) |

## The standard trial + $1/month deal (best for most)

The default path: a **3-day free trial**, then Shopify's standing promotion of **$1/month for your first 3 months** on a paid plan. It's not a "long free trial" so much as a long *cheap* runway — and that's usually better, because you can **sell and keep the revenue** the entire time. For validating a real store, three months of full functionality for a few dollars beats any locked-down extended trial. We break the mechanics down in the [complete free-trial guide](/blog/shopify-free-trial-2026-complete-guide) and the [$1/month deal explainer](/blog/shopify-1-dollar-3-months-deal-2026).

**Pick this if:** you want to actually launch and take orders while you evaluate Shopify.

## Partner development stores (unlimited — but you can't sell)

This is the "free Shopify forever" answer people are really searching for — with the catch that matters. A **Shopify Partner development store** is free, unlimited in number, and **never expires**. But it's built for *building*: the storefront stays password-protected, it can't process real customer orders (test mode only), and some paid features run in preview. When you're ready to sell, you **transfer it to a paid plan**. The full mechanics are in our [Partner Program guide](/blog/shopify-partner-program-guide).

**Pick this if:** you (or an agency/freelancer) want unlimited time to build and test *before* committing to a plan — and you don't need to take orders yet.

## The 1MBB 120-day trial (eligible Black-owned businesses)

The longest genuine free trial where you can also sell: **120 days**, via Shopify's **1MBB (1 Million Black Businesses)** program with Operation HOPE. It requires a business that is at least **50% Black-owned**, located in the **US, Canada, or UK**, and a short self-certified application. Four months of free runway with full selling is a real head start. Full eligibility and application steps are in the [120-day trial guide](/blog/shopify-120-day-free-trial-black-owned-businesses).

**Pick this if:** you qualify — there's no reason to leave four free months on the table.

## What about students, nonprofits, and other groups?

A common question, and the honest answer is: **not a longer trial specifically.** Shopify offers *discounts* and program pricing to some groups (education and nonprofit pricing, for example), but the extended-*time* offers are the ones above. If you don't qualify for 1MBB and you need to sell, your best "long trial" is simply the **$1/month runway** — available to everyone.

## So what's the actual "longest" trial?

It depends on the one question most guides skip: **do you need to sell during the trial?**

- **Building only, no deadline:** Partner development store — unlimited, free, but non-selling.
- **Selling, longest genuine free period:** 1MBB's 120 days, if you're eligible.
- **Selling, available to everyone:** the 3-day trial → $1/month for 3 months.

There is no route that is both *free forever* and *able to take real orders* — that's the trade-off Shopify designs around, and knowing it saves you from chasing a deal that doesn't exist.

For most people, stop hunting for a mythical long free trial and take the **$1/month-for-3-months** runway — it's cheap, you can sell from day one, and it's the fastest path to knowing whether a real store works for you.
