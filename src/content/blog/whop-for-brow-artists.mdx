## Name the service, not just the result

A returning maintenance appointment and a first-time design consultation ask different things of both artist and client. Tell the buyer what the appointment includes, how long to allow, and what needs to be agreed before the visit. Keep any professional suitability assessment outside the sales pitch.

This guide covers business administration for eligible cosmetic services. It does not give treatment advice or establish approval for permanent makeup or other regulated procedures. Review Whop’s [business restrictions](https://docs.whop.com/trust-and-safety/trust-safety-overview/what-is-not-allowed-on-whop) for your actual offer rather than assuming every beauty service is treated the same.

| Appointment | What the client should understand |
| --- | --- |
| Returning maintenance | Which existing service is being maintained |
| First visit | Whether consultation time is included |
| Consultation only | Whether any treatment happens at that visit |
| Different service category | Separate suitability, timing, and price requirements |

## Make a small payment easy to reconcile

Consider an illustrative $45 appointment with $15 paid ahead. The remaining service balance is $30 before other disclosed charges. Say whether the first payment reserves the slot, whether it is credited toward the service, and what happens if the appointment changes.

Whop’s [checkout links](https://docs.whop.com/manage-your-business/payment-processing/create-checkout-link) support one-time payments and pre-purchase questions. Use an appointment reference in your own records so a client’s initial payment is easy to match. A generic payment receipt is not a complete appointment history.

Do not charge the reservation payment once through a product and again through a paid calendar slot. Decide which part of the journey collects money. The [calendar setup guide](/blog/whop-calendar-bookings-guide) explains why this handoff matters.

## Leave room between clients

If a service occupies thirty minutes and preparation adds ten, three consecutive half-hour slots will overfill a ninety-minute window. Start with your real working time, then decide what availability you can safely offer. Use your own timings; this example only illustrates capacity math.

The official [Calendar Bookings walkthrough](https://whop.com/blog/calendar-bookings-app/) shows duration and availability settings. It does not prove that every specialist beauty scheduling feature exists. Test reminders, rescheduling, location instructions, and client access in your account before depending on them.

## Use a pilot to find confusing wording

Ask a few existing clients to read the proposed menu. Can they identify the correct appointment without messaging you? If they repeatedly select a maintenance slot for a first consultation, change the description or booking process before sending more traffic to it.

Keep private intake and service notes in a suitable system. Share aftercare instructions you are qualified to provide, but avoid adding automated advice simply because a client portal has space for it.
