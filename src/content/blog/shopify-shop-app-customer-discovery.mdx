Shop can be an additional discovery and purchasing channel for eligible Shopify merchants. Treat it as a channel to configure, inspect, and measure. Having a Shopify store does not guarantee that every product will appear in Shop or that discovery will produce a predictable number of orders.

## Separate Shop from Shop Pay

Shop is the consumer shopping experience and related sales channel. Shop Pay is an accelerated checkout option. A shopper using Shop Pay on your website is not, by that fact alone, evidence that Shop discovered the customer for you.

This distinction matters when you evaluate results. A payment-method report answers how an order was paid. A channel or acquisition report answers a different question. Decide which question you are investigating before reporting a growth win.

Shopify's [Shop channel overview](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/shop) is the starting point for the current configuration. Review the [merchant guidelines](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/shop/eligibility) and product restrictions before planning a launch around this channel.

## Is the Shop app free?

**For shoppers, yes:** Shopify's [customer-experience documentation](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/shop/customer-experience) describes a free app for supported iOS and Android devices. Downloading it does not make the products sold through it free.

**For merchants, the Shop channel is listed as free with no marketplace fees** in its [official app listing](https://apps.shopify.com/shop), checked October 2, 2026. That describes the channel, not the total cost of operating a store. Budget separately for your Shopify plan, payment processing, stock, fulfillment, and returns.

[Shop Campaigns](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/shop/shop-campaigns) is a separate paid advertising program with conversion-based charges. Keep campaign costs separate from organic discovery when reviewing profitability. A free channel listing does not mean a paid campaign is enabled or required.

## Run an eligibility and catalog check

The current [display requirements](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/shop/eligibility/requirements) include an active Starter plan or higher, activated Shopify Payments, a chargeback rate below 1% over the previous six months, and at least one eligible product. The Online Store must not be private, and password or age-verification apps can affect compatibility. Shopify also identifies Network Intelligence as a prerequisite in its channel overview; review that setting and its explanation before changing it.

Eligible merchants can appear in Shop even without installing the channel. Installing it provides management features; it is not a promise of visibility for every product. Shopify says eligibility changes can take up to 48 hours to appear in approval status.

| Check | Evidence to keep | Action if it fails |
|---|---|---|
| Store eligibility | Current status in the channel | Resolve the stated requirement |
| Product eligibility | Status for each priority product | Review restrictions and product data |
| Availability | Correct price and sellable stock | Fix the underlying catalog record |
| Customer promise | Delivery and return information | Align the listing with operations |
| Purchase journey | Completed supported test | Correct the exact failure before promoting |

Shopify maintains a separate [store display requirements page](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/shop/eligibility/requirements). Requirements can change, so use the current channel status and documentation instead of an old list copied into a spreadsheet.

Pick a small sample across your catalog: a simple item, a variant-heavy item, a sale product, and an unavailable product. Record what the customer sees. A clean homepage is not proof that every variant is ready to purchase.

## Improve information customers actually use

Write product titles that identify the item. Include dimensions, materials, fit, compatibility, and what is included where those details affect a buying decision. Keep photographs representative of the exact product and variant.

If a customer repeatedly asks whether an accessory is included, answer that question on the listing. If stock is wrong, fix inventory handling before rewriting promotional copy. Our [AI product FAQ review checklist](/blog/ai-product-faq-review-checklist) provides a method for turning real questions into accurate answers.

These are customer-experience improvements. We are not claiming that a particular title format or update frequency is a disclosed Shop ranking factor. Shopify does not promise that following this checklist will create a particular discovery result.

## Measure a channel experiment

Shopify's [Shop analytics guide](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/shop/analytics) separates orders placed through Shop from orders referred to your online store. It also documents a Shop Pay transactions report. Those are different measures, so label them explicitly in your review.

| Question | Evidence to check | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Were products seen in Shop? | Shop product impressions | A sale or a unique customer |
| Were orders placed through Shop? | Shop channel orders | That every buyer was new to your brand |
| Did Shop refer visits to the store? | Acquisition report filtered to UTM source `shop_app` | That every referred visit became an order |
| Did a shopper use accelerated checkout? | Shop Pay transactions | That Shop originally acquired that shopper |

Start in **Sales channels → Shop** for channel metrics. To investigate referred sessions, use **Analytics → Reports**, open an acquisition report such as Sessions by referrer, and filter UTM source to `shop_app`. Some Shop metrics may be unavailable until enough customers use Shop. Record missing data as unavailable, rather than as zero performance.

Choose a fixed review window and keep a dated record of changes. If you improve ten listings, record their identifiers and the changes made. Note promotions, stockouts, price changes, or major traffic campaigns that occur during the same period.

Track orders and net sales attributed to the channel where available, cancellations, returns, and contribution after variable costs. Compare similar products and periods, but describe a small sample as directional evidence. Five additional orders during a storewide sale cannot establish that a listing rewrite caused the increase.

For a hypothetical order with $50 collected, $20 product cost, $7 fulfillment, $2 payment costs, and $3 expected returns allowance, contribution is $18 before fixed costs and any additional channel or acquisition costs. Revenue alone would overstate the money available to operate the business.

## Keep the post-purchase experience consistent

A customer who buys through an additional channel still needs accurate tracking, responsive support, and a clear returns process. Assign responsibility for those jobs. Do not assume Shop handles your fulfillment or customer-service workload for you.

Use the [first-month store checklist](/blog/first-30-days-shopify-store-checklist) to connect acquisition with operations. Review results alongside the [app cost audit](/blog/shopify-app-costs-audit-guide) so new channel software has a defined job and measurable cost. Expand the experiment only after the products and fulfillment process work reliably.
