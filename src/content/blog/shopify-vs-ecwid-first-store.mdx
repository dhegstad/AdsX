Ecwid is worth evaluating when you want to add a managed store to an existing website. Shopify is worth evaluating when you want the storefront and store operations centered in Shopify. Both can support selling without replacing every existing web page: Ecwid has embedded store options, while Shopify offers Buy Buttons as well as full online stores.

Choose using the catalog, checkout, and operating requirements you actually have. This comparison reviews official documentation as of September 17, 2026; it is not a hands-on speed or conversion benchmark.

## Match the platform to the starting point

| Starting point | Ecwid question to test | Shopify question to test |
|---|---|---|
| Existing editorial or business website | Does the embedded store fit the current layout and navigation? | Is a Buy Button enough, or is a separate storefront preferable? |
| New standalone store | Does Instant Site cover the content and design requirements? | Does the selected full-store plan cover the required workflow? |
| Growing catalog | Which plan supports the product count and features? | Which apps and catalog setup are needed? |
| Digital products | Which Ecwid tier includes the required delivery capability? | Which delivery app or workflow meets the file and access requirements? |
| Mixed online and in-person selling | Is the desired POS integration available on the selected tier? | What Shopify POS features and additional costs are required? |

Ecwid's [existing-website guide](https://support.ecwid.com/hc/en-us/articles/115004678945-Ecwid-for-any-website) describes embedding a store, category, product card, or Buy Button. Its [Instant Site guide](https://support.ecwid.com/hc/en-us/articles/360020914939-Setting-up-your-Instant-Site-General-steps) covers building a site when you do not already have one. Shopify's [Buy Button documentation](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/buy-button) explains its embedded selling route.

## Compare the plan that meets your requirements

The current [Ecwid plan table](https://www.ecwid.com/pricing) lists Starter, Venture, Business, and Unlimited. Product limits shown are 10, 100, 2,500, and unlimited respectively. Digital goods appear from Venture; other requirements, such as staff access or particular sales channels, can push a store toward a higher tier. Confirm every must-have against the current table.

Do not compare an entry plan with a fuller store plan simply because both have the lowest displayed price. Shopify Starter and a full Shopify online store serve different setups; our [Starter versus Basic guide](/blog/shopify-starter-vs-basic-first-store) explains that decision.

Use the live [Shopify pricing page](https://www.shopify.com/pricing) and Ecwid quote for your region, billing interval, and tax treatment. Introductory offers and annual billing can change the cash due without changing the recurring cost after the offer ends.

## Separate platform fees from payment processing

Ecwid states that it does not charge its own transaction fees. Payment-provider charges are a separate cost. Shopify's additional third-party transaction fees depend on the payment route and applicable exceptions; Shopify Payments card processing is a different fee category. Use the [transaction-fee explanation](/blog/shopify-transaction-credit-card-fees-explained) to avoid comparing only one line of the bill.

Create a cost sheet with five rows: platform subscription, payment costs at your expected order mix, required apps, existing website hosting, and setup/maintenance. Keep one-time costs separate from monthly commitments.

For an original hypothetical comparison, if one complete setup costs $20 more each month and saves two hours of recurring work, the implied cost of that time saving is $10 per hour before considering other differences. Replace those inputs with observed work and actual quotes; neither platform is assigned those numbers here.

## Test the same catalog and order on both

Use five representative products, including the hardest variant, shipping case, or digital item. Check navigation, mobile product selection, discounts, payment, order handling, and the staff task that happens after purchase. Record failures and workarounds rather than judging a storefront from its demo homepage.

If you are moving an existing shop, separately inventory products, customers, orders, content, URLs, and integrations. Do not assume every record type transfers through the same importer. Use the [migration redirect worksheet](/blog/shopify-url-redirect-map-store-migration) for the URLs that already earn traffic.

## Which should you choose?

Shortlist Ecwid when keeping the existing website is a priority and its embedded store and plan limits fit the operation. Shortlist Shopify when a Shopify-centered storefront, app ecosystem, and operational setup better match the business you intend to run. If both pass the required tasks, choose the complete cost and maintenance burden you can sustain.

Before subscribing, complete the [launch checklist](/resources/shopify-launch-checklist) and a realistic budget. Neither platform supplies product demand simply because checkout works.
