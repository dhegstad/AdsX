Rubik Variant Images and SA Variant Image Automator address a broader job than assigning one photograph to a variant: showing a relevant set of media when a shopper changes options. The economical choice depends on your catalog, Shopify plan and required gallery behavior.

## Decide whether you need a gallery app

Shopify supports one assigned image per variant, with additional pictures in the product gallery. That can be sufficient if a shopper only needs a representative image and can browse the rest. A filtered set of photographs for each color is a different requirement. Shopify also points eligible Plus and Enterprise Commerce merchants to Combined Listings for grouped images across options; assess the catalog structure before assuming that is interchangeable with a variant-gallery app. [Shopify variant-image documentation](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/product-media/add-images-variants)

## Work through the catalog and labor cost

For a hypothetical Basic store configuring 80 products, twelve monthly SA charges total $178.80; twelve Rubik Starter charges total $300. Rubik's listed annual option is $200. These are subscription comparisons, not proof of equivalent results. On Grow, twelve SA charges total $358.80, so the ordering changes. Check billing cadence, taxes and any commitment before treating these arithmetic examples as an invoice forecast.

AI assignment can reduce repetitive setup only if the results pass review. Give an assistant an approved media manifest with product, option, image and shared-media labels. Ask it to flag ambiguous assignments rather than inventing which color a photograph represents. Measure correction time on a small sample before estimating catalog-wide savings.

## Make the trial answer a concrete question

Test a product with several colors, a shared size chart, a video and an unavailable combination. Check direct variant links, mobile switching, the selected cart item and behavior after disabling the app in a theme copy. Confirm how existing assignments are retained or exported before relying on a migration path.

Choose native assignment when it meets the requirement. Trial SA when Shopify-plan pricing fits your budget; trial Rubik when product capacity and its listed assignment tools fit the workload. Theme compatibility and review effort can outweigh a small subscription difference. These are direct app-listing links, not verified AdsX app referral links.

## Try the worksheet prompt

Illustrative prompt, not execution-tested.

> Compare the supplied Shopify plan, number of products requiring galleries, media types, current app prices and required behaviors. Calculate twelve monthly charges separately from annual offers; include setup and review labor. Create a pilot matrix for colors, shared media, video, unavailable variants, direct links, mobile and cart identity. Flag uncertain image assignments. Do not infer compatibility, migration support or conversion uplift from a listing.
