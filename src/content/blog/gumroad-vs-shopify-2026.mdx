> **Quick answer:** Gumroad takes a flat 10% + $0.50 of every sale and charges no monthly fee — brilliant for a few digital drops or testing whether an idea sells. [Shopify](https://shopify.pxf.io/c/6318547/3797171/13624) charges a monthly plan but only a normal card rate (~2.9% + 30¢), so once you're selling more than roughly $500/month it's usually cheaper — and you own a real storefront, your domain, and your customer list. Selling one or two files to your own audience? Gumroad is the smart start. Building a brand or catalog? Go Shopify — free trial, then $1/month for your first three months.

"Gumroad vs Shopify" is really a question about **stage, not features**. Gumroad is the fastest way to charge money for a file; Shopify is the platform you build a business on. Get that framing right and the choice mostly makes itself — here's how to tell which one you need right now.

## Gumroad vs Shopify at a glance

| | Gumroad | Shopify |
|---|---|---|
| **Fees** | 10% + $0.50 per sale, no monthly fee | From $39/month + ~2.9% + 30¢ card rate |
| **Best for** | A few digital drops, testing, quick payments | A real catalog, brand, and scaling |
| **Product types** | Digital files, memberships, some print-on-demand | Digital *and* physical, unlimited variants |
| **Storefront** | A simple hosted profile/product page | Full branded store, themes, your own domain |
| **Checkout** | Gumroad-hosted overlay | Shopify checkout + Shop Pay, on your brand |
| **Sales tax / VAT** | Handled for you (merchant of record) | You register and remit (Shopify Tax calculates rates) |
| **Ownership / portability** | Gumroad owns the storefront + URL | You own the store, domain, and customer list |
| **Multi-channel** | No | TikTok, Instagram, Amazon, Google, POS |

## What Gumroad actually is

Gumroad is the fastest way to sell a digital file. You upload a product — an ebook, template pack, preset, font, course, or membership — set a price, and share a link. No store to build, no monthly fee, and Gumroad acts as **merchant of record**, so it collects and remits sales tax and VAT worldwide on your behalf. That radical simplicity is the whole pitch: you can go from idea to a paid link in an afternoon.

What Gumroad is **not** is a store you own. Your product lives on a Gumroad-hosted page and URL, the storefront is minimal, and there's no real catalog, collections, or branded shopping experience. The 10% + $0.50 cut never goes down no matter how much you sell, and Gumroad keeps its fee even when you refund a customer. It's a payment link, not a brand.

## What Shopify actually is

Shopify is the default way to [build an online store](/start-a-shopify-store) in 2026 — millions of merchants, from one-product side hustles to billion-dollar brands. You get a real branded storefront on your own domain, a proper catalog with variants and collections, a checkout with Shop Pay's accelerated payments, and multi-channel selling that pushes your products to TikTok, Instagram, Amazon, and Google from one admin. Digital products are native: upload the file, mark it digital, and Shopify delivers the download automatically after purchase.

The trade-offs are the mirror image of Gumroad's. Shopify charges a monthly plan ($39 on Basic) and you handle your own sales-tax registration — Shopify Tax calculates rates, but Shopify isn't a merchant of record the way Gumroad is. In return you own everything: the store, the domain, the customer list, and the brand. Your variable cost is just the card rate (~2.9% + 30¢), not a 10% platform tax that scales with every dollar you make.

## Pricing: the comparison most reviews get wrong

This is where the two models genuinely diverge. Gumroad's fee is a **percentage of revenue that never drops**; Shopify's is a **fixed monthly fee plus a card rate** close to what any processor charges. Most reviews stop at "Gumroad has no monthly fee" and miss where the lines cross.

Gumroad's cut is 10% + $0.50 on every direct sale (30% if the buyer finds you through Gumroad's Discover marketplace). Depending on your payment setup the effective rate can land a point or two higher once card processing is counted — but 10% + $0.50 is the number to plan around.

Shopify Basic is $39/month plus 2.9% + 30¢ per transaction on Shopify Payments (2.7% on the $105 Shopify plan, 2.5% on $399 Advanced). New stores get a free trial and then $1/month for the first three months, so you can validate for almost nothing. Use a third-party gateway instead of Shopify Payments and there's a small surcharge (2% / 1% / 0.6% by plan).

Run the math at three revenue levels (Gumroad at 10% + $0.50, Shopify on Basic):

- **$500/month, ~10 sales:** Gumroad about $55; Shopify Basic about $57 ($39 plan + $14.50 card + $3). A near-tie — and any month you sell nothing, Gumroad costs $0 while Shopify still bills the plan. **Gumroad wins at this stage.**
- **$2,000/month, ~40 sales:** Gumroad about $220; Shopify Basic about $109. **Shopify saves roughly $110 a month.**
- **$5,000/month, ~100 sales:** Gumroad about $550; Shopify Basic about $214. **Shopify saves about $335 a month — over $4,000 a year.**

The crossover sits around $500–600/month in sales: below it, Gumroad's pay-only-when-you-sell model is hard to beat; above it, Shopify's flat fee plus a normal card rate pulls far ahead and keeps widening.

## The real decision: what are you selling?

**Gumroad is the better pick if:**

1. You have one or a few digital products — an ebook, template pack, preset, or single course
2. You're testing whether an idea sells before investing in a store
3. You sell internationally and want tax and VAT handled for you (Gumroad is merchant of record)
4. You value zero monthly cost and zero setup over owning a brand
5. Your sales come from your own audience and link, not from people browsing a store

**Shopify is the better pick if:**

1. You're building a brand and catalog, not just dropping files
2. You sell — or will sell — physical products alongside digital
3. You're doing more than ~$500/month and the flat 10% is now real money
4. You want to own your store, domain, and customer list (and email them directly)
5. You want to sell across TikTok, Instagram, Amazon, Google, or in person

The pattern we see with digital sellers runs through the whole creator cluster: a product takes off, the flat 10% starts costing hundreds a month, and the seller wants a brand and a customer list they actually own. You can [sell digital products on Shopify](/blog/shopify-selling-digital-products) natively and keep far more of each sale — and if you're weighing other simple tools, compare [Sellfy vs Shopify](/blog/sellfy-vs-shopify-2026), or if courses and memberships are the core, [Kajabi vs Shopify](/blog/kajabi-vs-shopify-2026) and [Shopify vs Teachable](/blog/shopify-vs-teachable-comparison). Audience-and-link tools win the first sale; Shopify wins the business.

## Bottom line

Gumroad and Shopify aren't really competitors — they're two different stages. Gumroad is the fastest, cheapest way to *start* charging for a digital product, and for a few drops or an untested idea it's genuinely the smart pick: no monthly fee, tax handled for you, live in an afternoon. But its flat 10% + $0.50 never scales down, and you're renting a payment link rather than building something you own.

Once you're selling steadily, want physical products in the mix, or care about owning your store and customer list, Shopify's economics and ownership win decisively.
