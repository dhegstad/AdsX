“Move GoDaddy to Shopify” can mean three different jobs: connecting a domain registered at GoDaddy, migrating a GoDaddy Websites + Marketing store, or moving a WordPress/WooCommerce store hosted by GoDaddy. Identify which one you have before exporting files or cancelling a service.

A domain-only connection does not require rebuilding an existing store. A store migration does. Choosing the correct path prevents a simple DNS task from turning into an unnecessary website project.

## Identify your current setup

| What you use at GoDaddy | Appropriate path |
| --- | --- |
| Domain registration only | Connect the domain to the Shopify store |
| Websites + Marketing with an online store | Move catalog data and rebuild the storefront |
| WordPress with WooCommerce | Follow the WooCommerce migration process |
| Email or other business services | Preserve those services separately from the website |

Check your account's products and current invoices. Record where the website, domain registration, DNS, and business email live. The same company can provide all four, but that does not make them one inseparable subscription.

If the current shop uses WooCommerce, go to the [WooCommerce migration guide](/blog/how-to-migrate-from-woocommerce-to-shopify). Its plugin, variant, and integration checks fit that software better than a website-builder migration checklist.

## If you only need to connect a domain

Create and review the Shopify store before changing the public domain. [Shopify's third-party domain instructions](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/domains/add-a-domain/connecting-domains) distinguish connecting a domain from transferring its registration.

Keep the registration at GoDaddy if that is your preferred arrangement. Follow the connection method offered by Shopify and use its current DNS values. A domain transfer can be considered separately; it is not a prerequisite for pointing a website to Shopify.

Save the original DNS records, identify the records used by email, and check the domain's final connection status. Test the website and mailbox separately. Our [domain and email checklist](/blog/connect-existing-domain-shopify-email-checklist) provides a compact change record.

## If you are moving a Websites + Marketing store

[Shopify's GoDaddy migration guide](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/migrating-to-shopify/migrating-from-godaddy) describes the product export and destination setup process. Use the documented method for the GoDaddy store product you actually use.

Before the export, inventory product names, descriptions, options, SKUs, stock, images, and public URLs. Retain a copy of the source data. Choose a small pilot containing your ordinary product and any unusual option or delivery requirement.

Review a transferred item in the storefront and in an order. Check price, image, availability, weight, and fulfillment details. A completed product import does not demonstrate that navigation, contact forms, or shipping rules have moved.

## Rebuild the pages and services deliberately

List every page that supports buying or customer service. Include delivery information, returns, contact details, product care instructions, and useful editorial pages. Copy only content you own or are entitled to reuse, and correct information that no longer applies.

Recreate the navigation around the actual product catalog. For a fictional maker with eight products and a long service page, the important test is whether a customer can distinguish a ready-to-ship product from a custom order. A generic theme demo does not answer that question.

If the old website accepts bookings, forms, or service requests, choose and test their destination workflows. Verify where each submission goes and what the customer receives. Keep a record of any historical submissions that the business still needs.

## Preserve useful URLs and email

Map valuable old pages to their closest new equivalents before connecting the domain. Use the [redirect guide](/blog/shopify-url-redirect-map-store-migration) for changed paths. Keep descriptions and product identities clear enough that an old link still makes sense after it redirects.

Shopify does not provide mailbox hosting; its [email-hosting documentation](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/domains/email-forwarding-and-hosting/email-hosting) explains the use of an external provider. Moving the website does not by itself move a GoDaddy mailbox into Shopify.

Review bundles before cancelling them. If email or another service depends on the subscription you plan to stop, arrange its continuation first. Do not cancel domain registration merely because the storefront has moved.

## Launch and reconcile

Record the final product and stock export time. Reconcile orders that arrived after that snapshot, then confirm the destination catalog and checkout. Run the [test-order checklist](/blog/shopify-test-order-before-launch-checklist), including notifications and the fulfillment process.

After the public switch, check the homepage, several old links, product pages, customer support route, and business email. Retain the source records needed for earlier orders and financial work.

## What should this project cost?

The domain-only path primarily involves connection and verification work, plus the subscriptions you intentionally retain. A store migration adds catalog cleanup, design, forms, integrations, and testing.

Ask for a quote broken down by those tasks. “GoDaddy migration” is too broad to price accurately. Define the current software and desired outcome first, then pay only for the work that outcome requires.
