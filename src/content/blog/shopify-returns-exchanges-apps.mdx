Choose a returns app by the cases your team needs to resolve: a refund, a size exchange, a damaged item, an international shipment or a return arriving after the normal window. A polished portal is useful only when its rules, charges and warehouse handoff fit those cases.

This comparison uses current public listings rather than claimed hands-on tests. It does not promise a universal exchange uplift or a fixed implementation time. The original article's Returnly recommendation and older pricing have been corrected.

## Returnly is no longer an option

[Loop's Returnly notice](https://www.loopreturns.com/returnly/) confirms that Returnly shut down on October 1, 2023. Do not use an old comparison that treats it as an available standalone product. If you are working with historical Returnly records, preserve them for reconciliation and confirm the current system responsible for any unresolved case.

A vendor transition also needs an open-case plan. Identify which system owns each request, where staff should respond, and what customers see when following an old link. Buying a replacement subscription does not automatically transfer every historical return or integration.

## Compare current listed plans

Prices checked November 9, 2026, in USD. Listings and contracts can change; check the final plan, usage charges and eligibility before subscribing.

| Provider | Public listed options | What to verify |
|---|---|---|
| Loop | Checkout+ listed free; Essential $155/month; Advanced $340/month | Funding model, labels, covered destinations and required exchange features |
| AfterShip Returns | Return Care listed free; Essentials $19/month; Premium $119/month | Shopper-funded terms, included returns, overages and automation tier |

The [Loop listing](https://apps.shopify.com/loop-returns) places automated policies, carrier rate shopping and workflows in Essential, and lists Shop Now, instant exchange and bonus credit in Advanced. Its free Checkout+ offer has a different model and feature package; investigate that model rather than assuming it is equivalent to a paid subscription with no other conditions.

The [AfterShip Returns listing](https://apps.shopify.com/returns-center-by-aftership) lists 20 monthly returns with Essentials and a $0.50 charge for each extra return. Premium lists 100 monthly returns and $1 for each extra return, alongside advanced workflows and other features. Return Care is described as shopper-funded. A zero subscription price does not by itself describe the full cost to your customer or business.

For a hypothetical 40-return month on the listed Essentials terms, AfterShip's base plus return overage is $29: $19 plus 20 × $0.50. That excludes labels, taxes, other charges and staff work. It is not a quote, and it does not establish which plan is appropriate. Never compare that subtotal with another provider's all-in bill.

## Test the cases that drive your choice

Build a small acceptance matrix before committing to a migration. For each case, document the expected customer option, charge, inventory effect, staff action and final financial result.

- A size exchange when the replacement is available and when it is sold out.
- A partial return from a multi-item order with a discount.
- A damaged item that needs a different disposition from a resaleable item.
- A request outside the standard return window that requires human review.
- An international order with the actual carrier and destination involved.
- A customer who abandons the portal and later contacts support.

Test with authorized controlled orders and the vendor's supported process. A demonstration of one uncomplicated domestic return does not establish that your warehouse integration or international workflow works. Ask which features require an additional tier, separate integration or custom agreement.

## Measure margin and workload, not only retained sales

An exchange can preserve a sale while adding shipping, handling, incentive and inventory costs. Track those costs alongside refunded revenue, exchange value and customer support effort. Store credit also creates an outstanding obligation; it is not automatically profit at issuance.

For a hypothetical $60 exchange, a $6 incentive, $8 shipping charge and $4 handling cost consume $18 before considering the replacement product's cost. This arithmetic is a planning example, not a claim about either app. Model your own cases using actual operational inputs.

Do not confuse financial reversals with physical units returned when reviewing outcomes. Use our [AI-assisted return-reason analysis](/blog/shopify-ai-return-reason-analysis) to prepare a traceable investigation, and review the source definitions before calculating a return rate.

## Make the switch reviewable

Keep your existing policy and case records, assign an owner for open requests, and agree on a rollback approach before changing the customer-facing route. Verify the portal, notification wording, staff permissions, warehouse status and final refund or exchange result. Record what was actually tested and what remains unknown.

Use the [app replacement cost worksheet](/blog/shopify-ai-app-replacement-cost) for subscriptions, usage fees and labor. The [merchant prompt library](/resources/shopify-merchant-prompt-library) can help structure the comparison, but an assistant cannot verify an app's behavior merely by describing it. Keep the service that meets your required cases at an acceptable total cost, even when another headline price looks lower.
