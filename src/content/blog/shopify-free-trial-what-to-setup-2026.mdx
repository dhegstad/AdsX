Use your Shopify trial to answer a practical question: can you build and operate the store your business needs at a cost you can support? Choose a small set of representative products, configure the essential ordering path, and record the gaps before adding optional apps or polishing every page.

## Record the offer in your account

Shopify's [trial documentation](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/intro-to-shopify/pricing-plans/free-trial) explains that trial length and promotional pricing can vary. Record the offer you actually receive, the trial end date, any introductory period, and the ongoing plan cost. Do not build a budget around a promotion from an old screenshot.

Separate the platform subscription from app charges, domains, payment processing, and fulfillment expenses. Use our [startup-cost calculator](/tools/shopify-startup-cost-calculator) to model your own assumptions. A free trial is a chance to evaluate software; it does not make every business expense free.

## Session one: build a representative catalog

You do not need your entire future assortment to learn whether the workflow fits. Choose products that expose the differences you expect to handle: a simple item, an item with variants, and a product with a delivery constraint.

For a hypothetical handmade-candle store, that could mean one standard candle, a scent variant, and a gift set. Record the information each needs before writing descriptions. A useful catalog trial answers whether the operator can maintain prices, availability, images, and options without confusing the customer.

| Item to check | Evidence to keep |
| --- | --- |
| Product information | A completed sample page without placeholder claims |
| Variants | Correct options, prices, and availability |
| Navigation | A clear route to the sample products |
| Delivery promise | Wording consistent with what the business can fulfill |
| Costs | Subscription and other expenses entered in the budget |

## Session two: inspect the ordering path

Work through the [test-order checklist](/blog/shopify-test-order-before-launch-checklist) using the testing method supported for your setup. Record what you checked rather than assuming a visible checkout means everything works. Check the intended destination, delivery option, inventory behavior, and payment configuration.

A successful simulated order is evidence about that tested path. It is not proof that every payment method, shipping destination, or app integration works. Keep unusual but important baskets on a small follow-up list.

For the candle example, test the ordinary single item and the gift set separately. If they use different packaging, the checkout assumptions may differ even though the storefront design looks the same.

## Session three: decide whether to continue

List unresolved problems and identify their owners. Is the issue missing product information, an unsupported requirement, an app decision, or an unfamiliar setting? Those require different responses.

Avoid installing several apps to solve a problem you have not defined. First state the needed outcome, then check whether the platform already supports it and what an additional app would cost. Our [trial evaluation scorecard](/blog/shopify-free-trial-evaluation-scorecard) provides a broader decision framework.

A useful decision record might say: “Catalog and ordinary delivery work; gift-set shipping still needs verification; the recurring cost fits the budget.” That is more informative than “the trial went well.”

## Launch when the required setup is complete

Shopify's [launch guide](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/intro-to-shopify/initial-setup/setup-prepare-for-launch) requires a selected plan, business address, and configured payment method with test mode off. Selecting a plan during a free trial does not start subscription charges before that trial expires, according to the guide. Check your account's terms and dates.

Public storefront access is a separate check. Follow our [private-mode launch guide](/blog/shopify-private-mode-launch-checklist) to inspect the store as a visitor after changing access. Do not confuse an admin preview with a public launch, or public access with search indexing.

If the fit is still uncertain, resolve the material question before committing more time or money. If it fits, [review the current Shopify trial offer](https://www.shopify.com/free-trial), record its terms, and use this checklist to make the evaluation concrete.
