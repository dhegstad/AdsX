> **Quick answer:** Kartra is a funnel machine for selling a few offers; Shopify is a commerce platform for building a store. If you sell products — physical or a growing digital catalog — [start with Shopify](https://shopify.pxf.io/c/6318547/3797171/13624) (free trial, then promotional pricing for your first months). If you sell one high-ticket course through webinars, Kartra earns its higher price.

Searches for "Kartra vs Shopify" keep climbing in 2026, and the comparison confuses people for a good reason: both platforms let you take money online, and both market themselves as the only tool you need. But they are built around opposite theories of how a sale happens.

Kartra assumes the sale happens in a **funnel**: a visitor lands on one page, watches one video, gets one offer, maybe an upsell, done. Shopify assumes the sale happens in a **store**: a customer browses products, compares, adds to cart, checks out, and — critically — comes back next month and buys again.

Pick the one that matches how your customers actually buy. Here's how to tell.

## Kartra vs Shopify at a glance

| | Kartra | Shopify |
|---|---|---|
| **Built for** | Funnels, courses, coaching, webinars | Online stores of any size |
| **Entry price (2026)** | ~$119/month | $39/month Basic ([$5 Starter](/blog/shopify-starter-plan-five-dollars-review) for link-in-bio selling) |
| **Free trial** | Trial available, then full price | [Free trial + promotional first months](https://shopify.pxf.io/c/6318547/3797171/13624) |
| **Product catalog** | A handful of offers | Unlimited products, variants, collections |
| **Checkout** | Single-offer pages with bumps/upsells | Full cart + Shop Pay accelerated checkout |
| **Email marketing** | Built in | Built in (Shopify Email) + apps |
| **Memberships/courses** | Built in | Via apps |
| **App ecosystem** | Minimal | 8,000+ apps |
| **POS / in-person** | No | Yes, built in |
| **Ceiling** | List-size pricing climbs fast | Scales from side hustle to [Plus](/blog/shopify-plus-vs-advanced-when-to-upgrade) |

## What Kartra actually is

Kartra is an all-in-one marketing platform: landing page builder, email automation, checkout pages, membership areas, video hosting, helpdesk, calendar booking, and webinar integration under one subscription. For a coach or course creator, that consolidation is the pitch — replace five tool subscriptions with one.

Its checkout is genuinely good *at what it does*: single-offer pages with order bumps, one-click upsells, and payment plans. That is the funnel playbook, and Kartra executes it well.

What Kartra is **not** is a store. There is no browsable catalog, no collections, no inventory engine, no shipping rates, no product reviews, and no app store to add any of it. Selling twenty products on Kartra means building twenty separate checkout flows nobody can browse.

## What Shopify actually is

Shopify is the default way to build an online store in 2026 — millions of merchants, from single-product side hustles to billion-dollar brands. You get a real storefront with themes ([free ones are genuinely usable](/blog/best-free-shopify-themes-2026)), a product catalog with variants and inventory, a checkout that supports Shop Pay's accelerated payments, shipping tools, tax handling, analytics, and an app store that covers everything Kartra bundles — email, memberships, courses, booking — plus thousands of things it doesn't.

The trade-off: Shopify is a platform, not a bundle. Email automation beyond the built-in Shopify Email, advanced funnels, and course hosting come from apps, some free and some paid. The [true first-year cost](/blog/true-cost-running-shopify-store-first-year) depends on the stack you assemble — though the same is true of Kartra once you outgrow its limits.

And funnels? Shopify handles them now too. Landing page builders, post-purchase upsell apps, and [checkout customization](/blog/agentic-checkout-optimization-shopify) replicate most of the Kartra playbook inside a store that can also do everything else.

## Pricing: the comparison most reviews get wrong

Sticker prices make Kartra look 3x more expensive — ~$119/month entry versus [Shopify Basic at $39/month](https://shopify.pxf.io/c/6318547/3797171/13624). But the honest comparison includes what each price buys:

- **Kartra's price includes** email marketing, funnels, memberships, and video hosting. Adding equivalents to Shopify via apps might run $30–100/month depending on your choices.
- **Shopify's price includes** an actual storefront, catalog, shipping, and POS — things Kartra cannot add at any price.
- **Kartra's pricing scales with your contact list.** Cross certain list sizes and you're forced into higher tiers, past $500/month, regardless of revenue. Shopify's plan price is flat; you [upgrade plans](/blog/shopify-pricing-2026-every-plan-real-cost) for better card rates and features, not because your email list grew.
- **Shopify's promotional onboarding** (free trial, then heavily discounted first months — see the [current deal](/blog/shopify-1-dollar-3-months-deal-2026)) means you can validate an idea for almost nothing. Kartra has no equivalent runway.

For a new business watching cash, that last point is usually decisive.

## The real decision: how do your customers buy?

**Choose Kartra if all of these are true:**

1. You sell 1–5 offers, mostly digital (courses, coaching, masterminds)
2. Sales come from launches, webinars, or booked calls — not browsing
3. Email sequences are your primary sales channel
4. You want one subscription instead of assembling tools

**Choose Shopify if any of these are true:**

1. You sell physical products, print-on-demand, or a digital catalog that will grow
2. You want customers to browse, search, and reorder — a brand, not a launch
3. You plan to sell in person, wholesale, or internationally someday
4. You want the option to add any capability later without switching platforms

The pattern we see with e-commerce clients: businesses that start on funnel platforms and find product-market fit almost always migrate to a real store within two years, because repeat purchases and catalog growth break the funnel model. Migrating *to* Shopify later is possible — we've covered [Etsy](/blog/etsy-to-shopify-migration-guide) and [Squarespace](/blog/migrate-squarespace-to-shopify-guide) migrations — but starting on the platform you'll scale on is cheaper than moving.

If you're still weighing creator-platform options, the same logic applies to [Kajabi vs Shopify](/blog/kajabi-vs-shopify-2026), [Shopify vs Teachable](/blog/shopify-vs-teachable-comparison), [Shopify vs Stan Store](/blog/shopify-vs-stan-store-comparison), and [Shopify vs Patreon](/blog/shopify-vs-patreon-comparison): funnel-and-audience tools win for a single offer; Shopify wins the moment you're building a catalog and a brand.

## Bottom line

Kartra is excellent at the narrow thing it does: selling a few offers through tightly controlled funnels, with the marketing stack bundled in. Shopify is the platform you build a business on — lower entry cost, unlimited catalog, real checkout, and an ecosystem that adds whatever you're missing.

If you're on the fence, the practical tiebreaker is risk: [Shopify's free trial and promotional first months](https://shopify.pxf.io/c/6318547/3797171/13624) let you test a real store for less than one month of Kartra. Set up your catalog, run the [first-30-days checklist](/blog/first-30-days-shopify-store-checklist), and see whether a store or a funnel fits how your customers actually buy.
