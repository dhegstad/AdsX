A $10 thank-you coupon costs nothing to issue today and can reduce margin on a future order. That timing makes a two-sided referral offer easy to misprice. Start with the friend’s first order, then model what happens when the original customer comes back.

## Follow the reward through fulfillment

Judge.me offers referrals on its Awesome plan. A one-way offer gives the friend a discount. The two-way configuration also rewards the original customer after the qualifying friend order uses the discount and is fulfilled. Inspect the order and discount usage when reconciling a reward. Someone opening the referral link is too early to count as an acquired customer. ## Compare the same hundred friend orders

Assume 100 qualifying orders with a $50 basket, a $5 friend discount and $25 in variable costs per order, including goods, fulfillment and processing. The resulting contribution is $2,000 before fixed costs. Both offers incur the friend discount. The extra decision is whether the original customer also gets a $10 coupon.

If 40 customers redeem it on purchases they would have made anyway, the extra discount reduces contribution by $400. At $20 contribution per additional friend order, another 20 orders would cover that difference **if they create no additional advocate redemptions**. For a continuing offer with the same 40% redemption rate, allow $4 expected advocate cost per new friend order. The net contribution becomes $16, so the steady-state threshold is 25 additional friend orders. That is 125 total versus 100 on the one-way offer. These figures assume unchanged baskets, costs and refund rates.

## Read the result after coupons have had time to mature

Track qualifying friend orders, fulfilled orders, advocate rewards issued, redemptions, refunds and contribution by cohort. Give both cohorts equal time to redeem. An advocate who places an otherwise incremental repeat order can contribute margin beyond the coupon expense. Without a credible baseline for repeat buying, show that upside separately. Include any incremental app subscription and staff time in the final decision.

Prefer the one-way offer when the friend incentive already brings buyers and little extra participation follows the advocate incentive. A two-way offer earns its budget when additional contribution from friend and repeat orders exceeds its cost. This exercise estimates a merchant’s own program economics.
