Choose **Fairing** if you have up to 200 monthly orders and want survey answers flowing into tools such as Klaviyo. Choose **KnoCommerce Starter** if you exceed Fairing’s free order allowance, need one survey with up to three questions, and a manual export fits your reporting routine. The choice changes again if you need to segment buyers or sync answers to several destinations.

## What would each app cost at your order volume?

These examples use advertised monthly USD prices checked November 11. Fairing prices the listed tiers by order volume. KnoCommerce lists its tiers by features, surveys and integrations. Confirm the price for your account before installing. [Fairing Shopify listing](https://apps.shopify.com/fairing) and [KnoCommerce pricing](https://knocommerce.com/pricing/).

| Monthly orders | Fairing | KnoCommerce Starter | Which requirement decides it? |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 180 | Free | $19 | Fairing includes integrations on the free tier. |
| 450 | $49 | $19 | A weekly CSV can justify Starter. A required automatic sync changes the comparison. |
| 900 | $99 | $19 | Compare the $80 monthly difference with the time spent moving and checking data. |

For example, a 450-order shop spends 20 minutes a week exporting and joining answers. At an assumed $30 per staff hour, four exports cost $40 in staff time. If Fairing’s supported integration actually eliminates that work, the $30 subscription difference could be worthwhile. Time a real export and check that the integration carries the order key and answer your analyst needs.

## When KnoCommerce Analyst or Pro makes sense

Starter lists one live survey, three questions and data export. Analyst lists two live surveys, unlimited questions and two concurrent integrations at $119 per month. Pro lists four live surveys, four concurrent integrations and custom audiences at $299. A team asking different questions of returning UK customers has a reason to examine custom audience rules. A merchant asking every buyer the same discovery question can start much smaller. Check the [KnoCommerce Shopify listing](https://apps.shopify.com/knocommerce) and the vendor’s plan matrix before budgeting for a specific integration.

Fairing’s listing includes order and lifetime-value analysis alongside survey answers. At 1,001–5,000 monthly orders its listed price is $149. For a store using Shopify Analytics or Sheets, decide which destination will hold the weekly report and check the fields that arrive there. A long integration list is only valuable when it covers that path.

## Run one decision through the survey

Suppose a coffee brand wants to know whether podcast listeners first discovered it through an interview. Ask “Where did you first hear about us?” Include podcasts, a friend, search, the brand’s actual paid channels, and an open “Other” choice. Asking what persuaded the buyer to order today answers a different question.

In a fictional week with 450 eligible orders, 90 distinct orders yielded an answer and 18 named podcasts. Report a 20% response rate and a 20% podcast share **among respondents**. The 360 unanswered orders have unknown discovery sources. Before moving budget, inspect answer wording, placement, repeat buyers and whether multiple submissions counted against one order. Use the [survey attribution setup guide](/blog/post-purchase-survey-attribution-setup-shopify) to compare responses with campaign reporting.

If neither shortlist fits, compare [OrderSurvey](https://apps.shopify.com/ordersurvey) for your required checkout placements, or consider a broader survey tool if you also need on-site feedback. Ask each vendor the same pilot question: can your analyst trace one answer to the correct order, export it and reproduce the weekly denominator? That is a practical acceptance test before the next billing cycle.
