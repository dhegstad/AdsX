# AdsX publication and organic growth plan

September 9, 2026 · Initial execution cycle: 12 weeks · Paid acquisition and directory budget: $0

## Business and success criteria

Build an ecommerce publication that attracts both future Shopify merchants and existing merchants who could use AdsX. The app is near completion but has not been submitted to Shopify for approval. The product is a free Shopify app for third-party ads on Thank you and Order status pages, with merchant revenue sharing. Its install availability and commercial terms are unconfirmed. Marketing must distinguish proposed capabilities from released capabilities.

The affiliate target is $3,000 per month. Dennis reports $600 earned in the preceding six months, approximately $100/month averaged over that period. This makes the target a 30-fold increase over that average, not a forecast for the next quarter.

Shopify's [affiliate earnings documentation](https://help.shopify.com/en/affiliates/earnings) requires qualifying full-price Basic, Grow, or Advanced plan referrals and describes $150 and $25 geography-dependent payouts. The signed Impact contract is authoritative. Twenty referrals produce $3,000 only when all qualify for $150 and remain payable. Trials and promotional-plan activations are intermediate steps; reversals and the payment lag matter.

### Funnel scenarios, not conversion-rate forecasts

Use eligible new-merchant landing sessions as the denominator, not all blog visits. The following are planning assumptions to replace with observed cohort data:

| Scenario | Session → affiliate click | Click → trial | Trial → qualifying referral | Sessions for 20 referrals |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Cautious | 5% | 10% | 15% | 26,667 |
| Working | 10% | 15% | 25% | 5,334 |
| Strong | 15% | 20% | 35% | 1,905 |

Formula: `required sessions = target referrals / (outbound CTR × trial rate × qualified rate)`; round up. At the working assumptions, approximately 534 affiliate clicks create 80 trials and 20 eventual qualifying referrals. With an 80%/$150 and 20%/$25 mix, expected payout is $125 and 24 qualifying referrals are needed. Attribution windows, cookie loss, country mix, and refunds can change these outcomes.

Current secondary metrics: engaged existing-merchant readership, email subscription completion, and consented Meta/Google/X audience growth where integrations are configured. App acquisition is deferred until the publication reaches hundreds of thousands of monthly visitors/clicks and the app is approved. Those are later App Store visits, installs, and activation; Shopify referrals are a separate business outcome.

## Editorial production system

Each backlog entry carries: target query, audience, intent, unique deliverable, topic, priority, source family, proposed internal destinations, conversion path, and status. No invented keyword volume, difficulty score, or ranking guarantee.

Workflow: discover → check existing intent → research primary sources → write → verify claims/calculations → check links and metadata → render → publish → measure. Record sources and verification dates. Release batches need:

1. A direct answer near the beginning and a useful result: decision table, worked example, checklist, or reproducible workflow.
2. Links to primary sources for changeable product, pricing, technical, or policy claims. Cite beside the claim; an unconnected bibliography is insufficient.
3. Clear distinction between documented capabilities, editorial recommendations, hypothetical scenarios, and actual AdsX results.
4. Accurate plan/region/version qualifiers and publication/updated dates. Refreshing a date alone is not an update.
5. A unique intent and at least two relevant internal connections, including its hub.
6. A CTA that serves that reader: Shopify signup for prospective merchants; useful tools, related reading, and eventually optional email resources for existing merchants. Defer AdsX install promotion.
7. Real author identity or the transparent editorial-team byline. A team byline must not claim unverified client outcomes or tests.
8. Passing content validation, TypeScript/build checks for implementation changes, and desktop/mobile visual review.

AI can assist research organization and drafting. It does not replace source verification. Google describes [scaled content abuse](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies) in terms of low-value manipulation, not a numerical publishing limit. Volume is useful when each page earns its place.

## SEO and AI discovery

Use clear topical navigation, crawlable HTML links, self-contained explanations, tables, source attribution, and Article/Breadcrumb structured data that matches visible content. Keep useful pages indexable, canonical URLs stable, the sitemap accurate, and RSS current. Use the article's actual `updated` date in sitemap lastmod.

Google states that [AI features use the existing SEO foundation](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features); no special AI schema or machine-readable file is required for eligibility. `llms.txt` may summarize AdsX accurately, but it is not a ranking guarantee. Search crawlers and model-training crawlers have different roles; allowing training is not a prerequisite for search visibility. Existing crawler access does not need indiscriminate expansion.

Use “AI search optimization” as the umbrella; AEO commonly means answer engine optimization, while agentic-commerce content concerns agents performing commerce tasks. Avoid building multiple pages for synonymous acronyms alone.

Track 20 stable question prompts monthly across accessible AI-search interfaces. Log date, engine/model when exposed, prompt, cited domain/URL, and whether AdsX was mentioned. Mark unrun prompts as unmeasured. Do not report a citation-rate baseline without running the checks. AI referrals and Google clicks are commercial signals; mentions alone are not revenue.

Dennis’s clarified sequence is publication traffic → Shopify affiliate income now → email and remarketing audiences → AdsX acquisition later. Hundreds of thousands of monthly visitors/clicks is the later distribution ambition, not a prerequisite for the first $3,000 of affiliate income. Google Search clicks, analytics users, sessions, and advertising audience sizes are distinct measures; report them separately.

Audit the existing GA4, Meta, Google Ads, and X tags before adding duplicates. The appropriate account/pixel IDs, enabled consent controls, and actual event receipt must be verified before claiming an audience is accumulating. Plan audiences by broad content interest and new-store versus existing-merchant intent; do not send email addresses, order details, or sensitive URL parameters in ordinary page-view events. A “seasoned pixel” means observed, usable measurement and audiences, not an assumed performance advantage.

Email phase: start with a merchant app-cost worksheet or store-launch checklist, a clearly described subscription, delivery confirmation, an unsubscribe route, and separate signup completion tracking. There is no working mailing list merely because an email field is visible. Use a free tier only after checking its limits and the existing account. Existing contact inquiries are not automatic newsletter consent. Until connected, offer the functioning RSS feed.

## Free directory and backlink program

Directories are a small distribution channel, not the basis for a traffic forecast. Submit where the actual publication, tools, or released app belong. Use brand-name anchors, accurate descriptions, and real destination URLs. Do not use purchased packages, automated mass submissions, fabricated reviews, paid upgrades, or forced reciprocal links.

Start with a verified queue of relevant directories and launch communities. Product Hunt is free but is a coordinated launch channel; don't burn the app launch on a nonexistent install. SaaSHub and AlternativeTo may suit existing working tools. Curlie is selective and excludes sites consisting largely of affiliate links, so publication eligibility must be evaluated honestly. BetaList is excluded: its current support page explicitly says all submissions are paid. Uneed is also excluded: its free waiting line is closed to new products. Fazier remains unverified. Blog/RSS directories require the same live cost check; Feedspot accepted the URL submission but then offered paid featured placement, which was declined. No backlink is verified.

For each entry record the cost source, destination, existing-listing search, submitted copy, login/verification requirements, submission date, receipt, accepted URL, and observable link attributes. A saved draft is not a submission; a submission is not an earned backlink. Check acceptance once after the stated review period; avoid repeated duplicate submissions. Limit initial effort to two hours/week and 3–5 qualified opportunities/week. A directory requiring account terms or identity verification may need Dennis to complete that step; prepare all fields first.

Beyond directories, make the publication worth linking to: free calculators with transparent equations, reusable merchant checklists, dated compatibility matrices, and original aggregate app/commerce findings when data exists. Publisher outreach or community answers can be planned, but messages to other people require explicit authorization.

## Product-specific dependency

The distinction between Thank you/Order status extensions and the separate post-purchase upsell surface matters. Shopify [documents the extension surfaces separately](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/checkout-extensions), and [App Store requirement 5.8.9](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/launch/shopify-app-store/app-store-requirements#5-8-post-purchase-apps) prohibits third-party ads in the post-purchase application surface. Meanwhile the public [Rokt Ecommerce listing](https://apps.shopify.com/rokt-ecommerce) advertises third-party confirmation-page offers. That establishes a comparable category; it does not establish AdsX's approval or exemption. Confirm the specific extension targets, advertising use, privacy approach, and approval path with Shopify before public launch promises. Educational publication work can proceed now.

## Measurement and operating rhythm

Maintain weekly cohorts by URL, topic, and conversion intent. Review GSC pages/queries/countries, GA4 landing sessions and affiliate clicks, and Impact trials/full-price sales/locked commissions/payments. Keep page and CTA placement sub-IDs. Record separate tool use and email subscription events without sending personal data in analytics event parameters. Verify real event arrival before claiming end-to-end attribution works.

Weekly: release eight verified articles and two refreshes; inspect last week's links and reports; action 3–5 eligible free distribution opportunities; record results. Monthly: review 28-day organic/AI traffic, referral cohorts, geographic payout mix, repeat readership, subscribers, and accepted referring domains. Adjust allocation from measured results. Do not infer failure from a new page's first few days or perform bulk removal based on impressions alone.

The first execution closes when the plan/queue are saved, the first content batch and hubs are implemented, intent-aware navigation/CTAs work, checks pass, a reviewable deployment or PR exists, and directory work has an honest status record. The 12-week publishing cadence remains future work until runs are explicitly scheduled or requested.
