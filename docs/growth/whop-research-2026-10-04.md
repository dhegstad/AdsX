## Product/documentation feedback for Dennis

These are research observations, not reported production bugs:

1. The official new native-store announcement and older DTC webhook/custom-field documentation describe different routes. A current route-selection guide would help merchants avoid mistaking the older integration for the full product boundary.
2. Website cloning and Stripe subscription migration share the word “migration” but move different things. A visible scope checklist would reduce billing and access assumptions.
3. Financing overview pages use different provider counts and broad upfront-payment language alongside provider-specific settlement exceptions. A single dated source of truth would make merchant cost explanations easier.
4. Invoice buyers need the final service-fee and optional processing-fee treatment made clear before an invoice is sent.
5. A launch checklist for native ecommerce could document variants, inventory ownership, mixed carts, delivery, refunds, and supported integrations in one place. Our articles identify acceptance questions without inventing tested answers.
