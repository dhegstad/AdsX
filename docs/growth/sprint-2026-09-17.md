# September 17 publication and discovery release

This release adds eight practical Shopify guides and rewrites two existing articles. The new topics cover inventory-free business models, first-payout working capital, preorders, manual payments, shipping margins, returns budgeting, theme selection, and Shopify versus Ecwid. Each includes an original calculation, decision framework, or operational checklist. The article manifest records primary sources and review dates.

The transaction-fee rewrite separates payment processing from platform fees and corrects exceptions and worked examples. The AI-app rewrite removes unsupported citation promises and replaces unverified app details with current listings and a problem-based selection process. Both preserve their original publication dates.

## Discovery strategy

- Connect the new articles through topic hubs, related articles, and the startup cost calculator. Update sitemap dates only for pages changed in this release.
- Add free IndexNow notifications for newly published or materially updated pages. The command verifies the live domain key, page status, canonical URL, and indexing directives before sending one batch. Its default mode is a dry run.
- Keep Google sitemap submission and selective indexing requests separate from IndexNow. A receipt or request does not establish indexing, rankings, traffic, or AI citations.
- Give recent releases time to be crawled before interpreting their search performance. Prioritize useful additions to existing subject areas and corrections to older articles over repeating the same search intent across more URLs.
- Keep the nightly recommendation prompt aligned with the owner-maintained publication strategy. Remove obsolete consulting positioning and unsupported assumptions about AI traffic.

Email work remains paused. No paid directories, advertising, or model calls are required for this release. AdsX app installation promotion remains deferred.
